01 · The Ship

A floating destination.

The current concept takes inspiration from the grand, classic ocean liners: a distinctive black hull, elegant cream-colored upper decks, four prominent funnels, and generous promenade spaces—reimagined with modern comfort and safety. The Atlantic is envisioned as more than transportation between ports. It is designed as a destination of its own—with spaces for relaxation, entertainment, dining, recreation, families, and premium experiences.

04 · Development

The journey starts now.

The Atlantic is currently a concept and planning project. The path toward a real vessel would require years of research, professional expertise, financing, engineering, construction, testing, certification, and regulatory approvals.

Phase 1 · Concept

Define the brand, ship identity, guest experience, and core ideas.

Phase 2 · Research

Study cruise operations, maritime requirements, shipbuilding, finance, and technology.

Phase 3 · Professional Team

Build relationships with qualified maritime, engineering, legal, finance, and hospitality professionals.

Phase 4 · Design & Business Plan

Develop the ship concept, company model, financial plan, and operating strategy.

Future · Construction & Launch

With the necessary resources and approvals, move toward construction, testing, and eventual operations.
